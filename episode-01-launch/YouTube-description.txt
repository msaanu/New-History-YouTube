You have been chosen to become queen. Your father receives an appointment. Your mother receives a title. But how did the court decide it should be you?

Step inside Korea’s Joseon dynasty to explore the 1759 selection of Queen Jeongsun, King Yeongjo’s second queen. Through royal records and a carefully distinguished comparison with Lady Hyegyeong’s earlier selection as a crown prince’s bride, we follow family documents, successive selections, remembered preparations, and a criticism that brought a royal order against its author.

This episode asks: when a court chose a royal bride, how much did the decision change the lives of her family as well?
